How rare is the Tanzanite fang from Zulrah?

Tanzanite fang drops at 1/1,024 per main-table roll. Zulrah rolls its main table 2 times per kill, so the effective rate works out to roughly 1/512 kills.

How many Zulrah kills for the pet on average?

The snakeling pet drops at 1/4,000, independent of every other roll — so on average, 4,000 kills, though it can drop on kill one or take far longer.
